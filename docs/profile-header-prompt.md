# Banner del perfil y registro de diseño

La ilustración del banner es la imagen adjunta seleccionada por el usuario. Se copió directamente al repositorio conservando los bytes originales. El README presenta ahora una variante animada basada en el SVG que proporcionó el usuario; la imagen estática sigue disponible como alternativa de movimiento reducido.

Recursos: [imagen original](../assets/profile-header.png), [SVG original del usuario](../assets/profile-header.svg) y [banner animado](../assets/banner-animado.svg). Los detalles de animación y presentación están en [diseño del perfil](./diseno-perfil.md).

La imagen muestra una habitación con ordenador y libros de programación, pósteres de rock, gatos, detalles de Viego y Teemo y un retrato de un gato alado.

Referencias temáticas: [Viego](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/viego/) y [Teemo](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/champions/teemo/), de League of Legends.

## Registro de la variante anterior

El siguiente prompt corresponde a una propuesta anterior generada con la herramienta integrada ImageGen. El banner actual es el archivo adjunto proporcionado por el usuario.

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: finished panoramic illustrated GitHub README header, approximately 3:1 aspect ratio, full bleed.
Input image 1 is the user's reference and the target visual direction. Closely preserve its composition, visual storytelling, object placement, cozy atmosphere, and 2D illustration style. The requested change is to give this same scene a predominantly DARK NAVY BLUE palette with controlled spectral green accents.
Scene and composition: a cozy rock-music and gaming bedroom/studio viewed at a slight angle. On the LEFT, a wooden desk with a monitor, keyboard, computer tower with a soft green light, small speakers and headphones; an orange tabby cat sleeps on the corner of the desk. A padded gaming chair sits center-left. The back wall displays worn taped-up rock band posters, arranged as in the reference. On the RIGHT, the main character is a lovely black cat sitting on the floor, facing the viewer, with soft fluffy fur, expressive green eyes and a small spectral green crown inspired by Viego. Lean Viego's distinctive long glowing green spectral sword against the wall beside the cat. A green Teemo scout hat with round reddish goggles rests on the floor in front of the posters. A second orange tabby kitten walks at the bottom right beside a small guitar amplifier. Include the vinyl records, sleeves, cassette tapes, books and a few cable loops scattered naturally on the wooden floor as shown in the reference.
Exactly three cats total: one orange cat asleep on the left desk, the main black crowned cat sitting on the right, and one small orange walking kitten on the floor. Keep them adorable, relaxed and anatomically natural. Preserve the black cat's dark fur and green eyes/crown rather than adding human hair or a human face.
Rock posters: retain the reference's bands, with carefully drawn simple concert/album-style imagery and legible band names "HÉROES DEL SILENCIO", "NIRVANA", "MY CHEMICAL ROMANCE", "BRING ME THE HORIZON"; a smaller "TWENTY ONE PILOTS" poster may appear at the upper right as in the reference. No added lyrics or large new words.
Style: hand-drawn 2D comic / cozy lo-fi illustration, confident dark ink outlines, subtle cross-hatching and printed-paper / halftone grain, muted flat colors, simple dimensional shading. Follow the provided reference very closely. The cats and the whole room should feel drawn, not like realistic animals or a glossy 3D render. Retain the casual charming room full of a music and gaming fan's belongings.
Palette and light: dominant deep midnight blue / navy walls (#091426, #10213a), desaturated denim-blue furniture accents and subdued violet shadows. Keep the wood, beige/cream posters, warm orange cats and a cozy monitor light to balance the dark room. Viego's green crown, eyes and sword and the Teemo hat add small, crisp emerald / spectral-teal accents; they must not turn the entire room green. Keep enough brightness and contrast for every important object to remain readable in a narrow GitHub banner.
Typography constraints: NO personal-name overlay, NO "Frontend & Full Stack", NO React / TypeScript / Web line, no technology badges, no code, no empty typography panel. The whole width is the illustrated room. Only the small existing rock poster band names appear as environmental text.
Avoid: photorealistic fantasy splash art, 3D rendering, large ornate silver lettering, giant portals, outdoor forests, excessive neon, huge glowing eyes, threatening expressions, or a collage of separate icons. Make the final image look like a faithful dark-blue illustrated variation of the user's reference.
```
